Our Story

Built out of frustration with how things worked.

I started SmartBizSystems after watching too many Kenyan businesses lose leads to slow WhatsApp responses, waste hours on manual follow-ups, and spend on ads with no system to capture the s.

Today, SmartBizSystems has helped 34+ businesses across Kenya automate their operations, rank on Google, and convert more leads — without hiring more staff.
