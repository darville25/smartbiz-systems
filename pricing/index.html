FAQ

Common pricing questions

Can I pay via M-Pesa?
Yes — M-Pesa is accepted for all packages. We'll send you a payment request via M-Pesa Paybill or Till after the proposal is agreed. For monthly retainers, we can set up automated monthly payments.
Are there any hidden fees?
No. The prices listed are what you pay. We'll always discuss third-party tools (like WhatsApp API fees from Meta, or ad budgets) upfront — but our management fee is fixed. No surprises after you sign.
Do I own everything after the project ends?
100%. Every website, automation workflow, and campaign we build belongs to you. We hand over all logins, credentials, and source files on project completion. Zero lock-in — we earn your continued business through s, not contracts.
How quickly will I see s?
Automation s are immediate — your bot starts responding to leads from day one. SEO typically shows ranking movement in 60–90 days and significant traffic in 3–6 months. Paid ads usually show s within the first 2 weeks as we optimise campaigns. Most clients break even within 1–3 months.
Can I start with one service and add more later?
Absolutely. Most clients start with WhatsApp automation or a website, then add SEO or paid ads once they see s. We'll build everything to work together from day one so adding services is seamless.
Do you work with businesses outside Nairobi?
Yes — we work with businesses across Kenya (Mombasa, Kisumu, Nakuru, Eldoret, Thika) and East Africa (Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda). All work is delivered remotely. We communicate via WhatsApp and video calls.
