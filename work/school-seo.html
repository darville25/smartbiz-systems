The Problem → The Fix

What was holding them back. What we changed.

Before SmartBizSystems
Parents couldn't find the school on Google
  • Website buried on page 4+ for all target keywords
  • No schema markup — no rich s, no star ratings in SERP
  • Google Business Profile incomplete — wrong hours, no photos, no reviews
  • Page titles and meta descriptions missing or duplicated across pages
  • Site loading in 8+ seconds on mobile — Google penalising ranking
  • Zero backlinks from local Kenyan education or news websites
  • No content strategy — site hadn't been updated in 14 months
After SmartBizSystems
Ranking #1 for every major admissions keyword
  • #1 Google ranking for "school admissions Nairobi", "best schools Westlands" and 16 other keywords
  • Full schema suite — EducationalOrganization, FAQPage, BreadcrumbList, LocalBusiness
  • Google Business Profile fully optimised — 4.8★ rating visible in search
  • All page titles, metas, and H1s rewritten with target keywords
  • Page speed improved from 8.2s to 1.9s on mobile (Core Web Vitals: all green)
  • 14 new backlinks from Kenyan education directories and local news
  • 4 new SEO-optimised pages published targeting admissions search intent

What We Actually Did

The full SEO system, step by step.

No shortcuts. No black-hat tactics. Just a methodical SEO build that Google rewards and parents can find.

Full technical SEO audit
Crawled the entire site with Screaming Frog — identified 47 technical issues: broken links, missing metas, duplicate titles, slow images, and render-blocking scripts.
Screaming Frog · GSC · PageSpeed Insights
Core Web Vitals — 8.2s → 1.9s
Compressed all images (avg 78% size reduction), eliminated render-blocking CSS, enabled caching, and moved to a faster Kenyan-region hosting stack.
Speed optimisation · LCP fix
Full schema markup suite
Implemented EducationalOrganization, FAQPage, BreadcrumbList, Event, and LocalBusiness schema. Rich s appeared in Google within 3 weeks of deployment.
JSON-LD · Google Rich s
On-page content optimisation
Rewrote all page titles, H1s, meta descriptions, and body copy targeting 22 high-intent Kenyan education keywords. Published 4 new SEO pages targeting admissions queries.
Keyword mapping · Content strategy
Google Business Profile rebuild
Completed the profile from scratch — categories, services, hours, photos, Q&A, and a review acquisition strategy that got them to 4.8★ within 60 days.
GBP · Local SEO · Maps pack
Local backlink building
Secured 14 backlinks from Kenyan education directories, local parent forums, and a feature in a Nairobi lifestyle publication — building domain authority in the Kenya market specifically.
