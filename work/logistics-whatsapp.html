The Problem → The Fix

What was broken. What we built.

Before SmartBizSystems
Leads were slipping through every day
  • WhatsApp enquiries replied to hours later — or not at all
  • Night-time and weekend leads lost completely
  • Staff manually typing the same quote information 30+ times per day
  • No follow-up system for leads who didn't book immediately
  • M-Pesa payments received with no automatic confirmation or booking update
  • No visibility into how many leads were coming in vs converting
After SmartBizSystems
A system that works while the team sleeps
  • Instant WhatsApp reply to every enquiry — 24/7, including weekends
  • Bot qualifies the lead, gives a quote, and confirms the booking automatically
  • M-Pesa payment triggers automatic confirmation and driver assignment notification
  • Leads who don't book get a 3-step follow-up sequence over 48 hours
  • Every lead logged to HubSpot CRM automatically
  • Weekly dashboard showing leads, conversions, and revenue by channel
The System We Built

Every step of the automation, explained.

A 6-step WhatsApp automation flow that handles the entire customer journey from first enquiry to confirmed booking and payment — no human required.

Lead sends first WhatsApp message
Any message to the business number triggers the flow instantly. Responds in under 5 seconds, 24/7.
Meta WhatsApp Business API
Bot qualifies the lead with 3 questions
Cargo type, weight, and route. Answers are captured and logged. Takes under 2 minutes.
Conversational flow (Make.com)
Instant quote sent automatically
Quote generated from a pricing matrix based on the lead's answers. No human calculation needed.
Dynamic pricing logic
Booking confirmation + M-Pesa payment
Lead confirms, receives M-Pesa payment instructions. Payment triggers driver notification automatically.
M-Pesa Daraja API
3-step follow-up for non-bookers
Leads who don't complete get a follow-up at 2hrs, 24hrs, and 48hrs. Recovers ~20% of abandoned enquiries.
Automated re-engagement
Every lead synced to HubSpot CRM
Full lead record, conversation transcript, quote value, and booking status automatically created in HubSpot.
HubSpot CRM sync
