AI Automation

AI automation means replacing manual, repetitive tasks in your business with intelligent systems that run 24/7 without your involvement. For a Kenyan SME, this typically means things like: automatically responding to WhatsApp enquiries, sending M-Pesa payment confirmations, following up on leads who didn't buy, reminding clients about appointments, and syncing everything into your CRM.

Instead of you — or your staff — doing the same thing 50 times a day, the system does it every time, instantly, without errors or delays.

SEO & Digital Marketing

For local/maps SEO (Google Business Profile), most clients see movement within 4–8 weeks. For organic keyword rankings, meaningful traction typically comes at 60–90 days, with compounding growth from month 3–6.

Kenya's digital landscape is less saturated than Western markets — which means good SEO moves faster here. We helped one school go from 200 to 4,500 monthly visitors in 90 days, and ranked Modern Homes Interiors #1 for their main Nairobi keywords in 6 months.

Schema markup is structured data you add to your website that tells Google exactly what your business is, what you offer, your reviews, your location, and your FAQs — in a format Google can read directly. It helps you appear in rich s (star ratings, FAQ dropdowns, featured snippets) that take up more space on search s.

Do you need it? Yes — especially in Kenya where many local competitors don't use it, giving you a fast-mover advantage. We implement LocalBusiness, Service, FAQPage, and Review schema as standard on all SEO engagements.

Any agency that guarantees specific rankings is misleading you — Google's algorithm is controlled by Google, not us. What we do guarantee is:

  • Full technical SEO implementation to the highest standard
  • Monthly reporting showing real ranking movement, traffic, and leads
  • Continuous optimisation until we achieve strong s

Our track record speaks: 34+ clients, a school ranked #1 for admissions keywords, an interiors firm ranked #1 for Nairobi keywords. We are s-obsessed — just not in a way that involves false promises.

SEO builds long-term organic visibility — it takes 2–6 months to see s but delivers free traffic indefinitely once it ranks. Google Ads delivers traffic immediately but stops the moment you stop paying.
