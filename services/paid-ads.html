Common Ad Mistakes

Why Your Ads
Aren't Working

These are the 5 reasons most Kenyan businesses waste their ad budget.

Wrong audience targeting
Targeting all of Kenya instead of the 3km radius around Westlands where your customers actually are.
Sending ads to your homepage
Homepages have too many distractions. Dedicated landing pages convert 3–5× better than sending paid traffic to your main site.
Optimising for impressions, not sales
High reach and low CPM looks good in reports but means nothing if nobody buys. We optimise for conversions and ROAS only.
No tracking = no learning
Running ads without Meta Pixel or Google conversion tracking means you're flying blind. You can't optimise what you can't measure.
Slow lead follow-up
Leads go cold in minutes. If you're not following up via WhatsApp within 5 minutes of a form submission, you're losing 70% of your leads.

FAQ

Paid Ads Questions Answered

What's the minimum ad budget I need in Kenya?
We recommend a minimum ad spend of KES 30,000/month on Meta, or KES 40,000/month on Google, to generate enough data for meaningful optimisation. Below that, the algorithm doesn't get enough conversions to learn effectively. The management fee is separate from your ad spend — it goes directly to Meta or Google.
How quickly will I see s from paid ads?
You'll see leads coming in from week one — but meaningful ROAS optimisation takes 4–8 weeks. The first month is about learning: which audiences respond, which creatives convert, which landing pages work. From month two onwards, we scale what's working and the cost per lead starts falling consistently.
Do you handle ad creative (images, copy, video)?
Yes. We write all ad copy and source or design static image creatives in all packages. Video ads require you to provide video footage (we edit and format it). For the Growth and Scale packages, we also create carousel ads and dynamic creative variants for A/B testing.
Do you mark up the ad spend?
Never. Your ad budget goes 100% to Meta or Google — directly from your card or M-Pesa. We charge a flat management fee only. You always see exactly what you're spending on ads vs management, with no hidden markups or percentage-of-spend fees.
Can you take over and fix my existing campaigns?
Yes — this is actually where we deliver the fastest s. We audit your existing campaigns, identify what's wasting budget, fix the tracking, rebuild the account structure where needed, and implement our Kenya-specific targeting approach. Most takeover clients see CPL drop within the first 30 days.
Is there a contract or minimum commitment?
We recommend a minimum 3-month engagement to see proper s — paid ads need time to optimise. There's no lock-in contract beyond a 30-day notice period. Most clients stay 12+ months because the s justify the spend. You can pause, scale, or cancel with 30 days notice.
