Automation Logic

Emails Triggered by What Your Customer Does — Not the Calendar

The difference between email campaigns that annoy people and email sequences that convert is behaviour-based triggers. We build automations that respond to what your subscriber actually does — not just time delays.

  • Lead submits form → welcome sequence starts immediately
  • Opens email but doesn't click → follow-up with different angle
  • Clicks pricing page → sales sequence triggered immediately
  • M-Pesa payment received → onboarding sequence starts
  • No open in 60 days → win-back campaign triggered
  • WhatsApp opt-in captured → synced to email list automatically

FAQ

Email Marketing Questions Answered

Do Kenyan audiences actually respond to email?
Yes — significantly better than global averages when done right. Kenyan business email open rates for well-segmented, relevant sequences average 40–55%, compared to a global average of 21%. The key is personalisation and relevance to the Kenyan context, not generic templates.
What if I have a small email list?
Even 200 subscribers can generate meaningful revenue with the right sequences. A well-written welcome sequence to a small, engaged list outperforms a broadcast to a large cold list every time. We start where you are — the system scales as your list grows.
Do you write all the emails or do I need to provide content?
We write everything — every subject line, every email body, every CTA — in your brand voice and tailored to your specific industry and Kenyan audience. You review and approve before anything goes live. You'll never need to write a single email yourself.
Will my emails land in inbox or spam?
Deliverability is a core part of every setup we do. We configure SPF, DKIM, and DMARC records on your domain, warm up new sending infrastructure properly, and maintain list hygiene ongoing. Our clients typically achieve 95%+ inbox placement rates.
Can email work alongside WhatsApp — do I need both?
They work best together. WhatsApp handles instant, personal, conversational touchpoints. Email handles longer-form nurture, educational content, and broadcast campaigns. Together they create a multi-channel system where if a lead doesn't respond on one channel, the other picks them up. We sync both by default.
How do I grow my email list in Kenya?
We build the list growth system alongside the sequences — opt-in forms on your website, lead magnets (free guides, calculators, checklists), WhatsApp opt-in capture, and Meta lead ad integration. By the time your sequences are live, you'll have multiple active sources feeding subscribers into your list automatically.
