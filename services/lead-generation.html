How It Works

Your Lead Machine, Step by Step

We build the full funnel — attract, capture, nurture, convert. Every step automated and measurable.

01
Attract — Drive Qualified Traffic

We identify the exact keywords, audiences, and channels your ideal Kenyan customer uses — SEO, Meta Ads, Google Ads, or WhatsApp. Traffic comes in warm, not cold.

02
Capture — Landing Page + Lead Magnet

A purpose-built landing page with a compelling offer captures name, WhatsApp, and key qualification data. Conversion rates of 25–40% vs industry average of 8–12%.

03
Respond — Instant WhatsApp + Email

The moment a lead submits, they receive a personalised WhatsApp message (within 5 seconds) and an automated email sequence. You're the first to respond — every time.

04
Convert — Nurture to Sale

Leads who don't buy immediately are nurtured over 14–30 days with educational content, social proof, and timely offers. When they're ready to buy, your name is top of mind.

FAQ

Questions About Lead Generation in Kenya

How quickly can a lead generation funnel be live?
A Starter funnel — landing page, WhatsApp flow, and email sequence — can be live in 7–10 business days. Growth and Enterprise packages take 3–4 weeks depending on the number of pages and integrations required.
Do I need to run ads for this to work?
No. We build funnels that work with organic traffic (SEO, Google Business Profile, social media) as well as paid ads. We recommend starting with whatever traffic source your business already has, then scaling with paid ads once the funnel is converting well.
What CRM do you use and will I need to pay for it?
We primarily build on HubSpot, which has a free tier that covers most SME needs. We also work with Brevo and Zoho depending on your requirements. We'll recommend the right tool for your budget and team size during the audit call.
Can you integrate with M-Pesa payments?
Yes. M-Pesa payment integration via the official Daraja API is available in our Growth and Enterprise packages. When a customer pays via M-Pesa, we can trigger automatic confirmation messages, access grants, or CRM deal updates — instantly.
Will I own the funnels and all the data?
100%. You own everything we build — the landing pages, the CRM, the automation flows, and all your lead data. Zero lock-in. If you ever part ways with SmartBizSystems, everything stays with you and keeps working.
How many leads can I expect per month?
This depends on your traffic source and industry. Our funnels typically convert 25–45% of landing page visitors into leads (vs industry average of 8–12%). We'll provide realistic projections during your free audit based on your current traffic and ad budget.
