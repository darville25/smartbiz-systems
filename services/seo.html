Schema Markup

The Secret Weapon Most Kenyan Businesses Are Missing

Schema markup tells Google exactly what your business is, who you serve, and why you're trustworthy — in language machines understand. Businesses with schema markup get richer search s, higher click-through rates, and faster ranking improvements.

  • LocalBusiness schema — name, address, phone, hours
  • FAQPage schema — your answers appear directly in search s
  • Service schema — each service gets its own structured listing
  • Review schema — star ratings displayed in Google s
  • BreadcrumbList schema — improved site navigation in SERPs

How long does SEO take to see s in Kenya?
Most clients see initial ranking movement within 30–45 days. Significant traffic increases typically happen between months 2 and 3. Highly competitive keywords can take 4–6 months. We'll give you a realistic timeline based on your specific industry and competition during the audit.
What is schema markup and do I need it?
Schema markup is code added to your website that helps Google understand your business — your name, location, services, reviews, and FAQs. Businesses with schema markup appear with rich snippets (star ratings, FAQ dropdowns) in search s, which dramatically increases click-through rates. Yes, you need it.
