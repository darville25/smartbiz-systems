3. Step-by-Step Implementation for Kenyan Importers

Step 1: AIS Ship Tracking and Geofencing

Don't wait for your agent's call. Use real-time satellite AIS (Automatic Identification System) data to see exactly where the vessel is.

The Kenyan Strategy:
• Use professional APIs from MarineTraffic or VesselFinder. Enter the IMO number of the ship provided on your Bill of Lading.
• Set a digital "Geofence" around the Port of Mombasa. Receive an automated WhatsApp alert the moment the ship enters Kenyan territorial waters.
• This gives you a critical 48-hour head start to finalize your customs clearing in Nairobi before the ship even berths.

Step 2: Automating iCMS and KenTrade Status Polling

The iCMS (Integrated Customs Management System) is the "brain" of Kenyan imports. In 2026, manual portal checking is obsolete.

The Workflow:
• Integrate your internal systems with a KenTrade Single Window API bridge.
• Monitor the "Declaration Status" in real-time (e.g., Lodged, Accepted, Paid, Released).
• If a declaration stays in "Lodged" status for more than 24 hours without moving to "Accepted," your system should automatically trigger a Slack or WhatsApp notification to your clearing agent to demand an explanation.

Step 3: M-Pesa G2B API for Instant Duty Settlement

Waiting for a bank to process a KRA payment registration number (PRN) can take hours, sometimes pushing you into an extra day of storage fees.

The Modern Flow:
• Your tracking tool extracts the exact duty amount from the KRA valuation response.
• It triggers an M-Pesa STK Push to the authorized director's smartphone.
• Upon PIN entry, the payment is settled via the KRA Paybill (222222) instantly, and the system polls for the "Release Order" which is triggered the moment the KRA system registers the funds.

Step 4: Last-Mile Delivery & SGR Integration

The container leaving the port is only the halfway mark. Whether using the Standard Gauge Railway (SGR) to Nairobi ICD or long-haul trucks, visibility is vital.

The Checklist:
• Integrate with Fleet management software to monitor the truck's speed and location along the Mombasa-Nairobi highway.
• Use "Smart Seals" that send a notification if the container is opened before reaching your warehouse in Industrial Area or Eastleigh.
• Provide your B2B customers with a tracking link so they can prepare their offloading teams, reducing "idle truck time" charges.

4. Frequently Asked Questions: Kenyan Import Logistics 2026

What is the 'Free Period' for storage at Mombasa Port in 2026?

Typically, KPA provides 4 free days for domestic containers. However, this includes weekends and public holidays. Automated tracking ensures you lodge your documents the moment the manifest is uploaded, usually 48 hours before arrival, to maximize these days.

How does the iCMS differ from the old Simba system?

iCMS is completely paperless and integrates directly with the shipping line manifests. This means tracking tools can "see" your goods much earlier than they could under the manual Simba system.

Can I pay KRA duties above KES 150,000 via M-Pesa?

Yes, with the 2026 M-Pesa limit increases and G2B integration, businesses can settle significantly higher duty amounts via the official government Paybill, though higher amounts may require multiple transaction batches or bank-integrated STK pushes.
