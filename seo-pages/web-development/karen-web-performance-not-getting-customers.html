Why should I care about "Core Web Vitals"?

In 2021, Google made "Page Experience" a major ranking factor. Core Web Vitals are the metrics Google uses to measure how fast and stable your site is. If your competitors in Karen have better scores than you, Google will push them to the top and bury you. We specialize in optimizing these scores to give you an unfair advantage in search results.
