Visibility FAQ

Common SEO Questions for Upper Hill Firms

Can you guarantee #1 ranking on Google?

No reputable agency can guarantee a #1 ranking because Google controls the algorithm. However, we guarantee that we follow 100% "White Hat" SEO practices that consistently deliver Page 1 results for our clients. We focus on the KPIs that matter: organic traffic and lead conversions.

What is the difference between SEO and Google Ads (PPC)?

Google Ads are paid listings; you pay every time someone clicks. SEO is organic; you earn your spot through authority and relevance. While Ads provide instant traffic, SEO provides long-term, sustainable growth with a much lower cost-per-lead over time. For Upper Hill firms, we often recommend a hybrid approach.

How do I know if the SEO is working?

We provide monthly transparency reports. You will see your keyword rankings moving up, your organic traffic increasing, and—most importantly—an increase in tracked WhatsApp enquiries and lead form submissions through your website.
