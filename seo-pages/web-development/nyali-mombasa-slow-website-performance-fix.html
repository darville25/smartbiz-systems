The Performance Killers

What is Making Your Nyali Site So Slow?

Many business owners in Mombasa are surprised to learn that their "beautiful" website is actually a technical disaster. Here is what we typically find during our Nyali performance audits:


Unoptimized Media

That 5MB photo of the sunset at Nyali Beach? It's killing your conversion. We use WebP compression and "Lazy Loading" to ensure images only load when they are about to be seen, keeping the initial page load lightning fast.

Poor Hosting Choice

If your website is hosted on a cheap server in Europe, every request has to travel thousands of miles to reach a phone in Mombasa. We use localized CDNs (Content Delivery Networks) to serve your site from the closest possible node.

Bloated Code

Many "developers" use pre-made templates with thousands of lines of unnecessary code. We "minify" CSS and JavaScript, stripping away the weight so your site can fly on Safaricom 4G.

Speed FAQ

Common Performance Questions in Mombasa

Can I keep my current design but make it faster?

In 90% of cases, yes. We can perform a "Performance Overlay" where we optimize the underlying code and assets without changing the look of your site. However, if your site was built with a very heavy page builder, a lightweight rebuild might be more cost-effective in the long run.

Why is my site fast on my office Wi-Fi in Nyali but slow on my phone?

This is a common illusion. Your office Wi-Fi has high bandwidth and your browser has likely "cached" (stored) the site. A first-time visitor on 4G in Bamburi doesn't have those advantages. We test your site using "Throttled" connections to simulate real-world Mombasa user conditions.

What is a good load time for a Kenyan website?

Ideally, your site should be "Interactive" in under 2 seconds. The total load time should not exceed 3.5 seconds on a standard 4G connection. Anything beyond that and you start losing a measurable percentage of your traffic.
