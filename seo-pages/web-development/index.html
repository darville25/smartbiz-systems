Common Questions

Web Development FAQ — Kenya

What Kenyan business owners ask before getting started.

How much does a website cost in Kenya?

Website packages start from KES 30,000 for a 5-page startup site, KES 75,000 for a corporate site with CMS, and KES 150,000+ for ecommerce with M-Pesa. All packages can be paid via M-Pesa and include mobile-first design, WhatsApp integration, and basic SEO.

How long does it take to build a website?

Startup websites are delivered in 5–7 days. Corporate sites take 10–14 days. Ecommerce with M-Pesa integration takes 3–4 weeks. Everything is delivered remotely across Kenya — no office visit required.

Do I own the website after it's built?

100%. You own the domain, hosting account, and all files from day one. SmartBizSystems has zero lock-in — if you ever want to move or change providers, you take everything. We build it, you own it.

Will my website accept M-Pesa payments?

Yes. We integrate the M-Pesa Daraja API into ecommerce and service websites — customers pay on your site and receive automatic confirmation messages. Built into the ecommerce package and available as an add-on for other packages.

Will my website show up on Google?

Every site ships with technical SEO foundations — meta tags, schema markup, mobile optimisation, and fast loading speed. For ongoing Google rankings, we recommend pairing with our SEO retainer from KES 40,000/mo.

Can you integrate WhatsApp into my website?

Yes — every website we build includes WhatsApp CTA integration. Visitors can start a WhatsApp conversation with one tap, with pre-filled message text so you know exactly what they're enquiring about. This is included in all packages.
