Why Kenyan Healthcare Providers are Moving to WhatsApp First

In the fast-paced urban centers of Kenya, patients no longer want to wait on hold to book a dental checkup or a consultation. Whether you are a specialist clinic in Upper Hill, a dental practice in Nyali, or a multi-wing hospital in Kisumu, your patients are already on WhatsApp. By providing an Automated Patient Concierge, you meet them where they are comfortable, resulting in higher trust and significantly lower administrative overhead.

Sealing the Revenue Leak: The No-Show Problem

The biggest financial drain for private clinics in Kenya is the "No-Show." When a patient misses an appointment without notice, your facility loses the consultation fee and the doctor’s valuable time. Our Automated Multi-Step Reminders send personalized messages 24 hours and 2 hours before the appointment. If a patient can't make it, the bot offers an instant "Reschedule" button, immediately filling that empty slot with another waiting patient.

AI Triage: Answering FAQs Before They Hit the Reception

"Do you accept NHIF?" "What are your consultation fees?" "Where are you located?" These questions flood your reception desk every day. Our AI-powered bots are trained on your specific practice data. They answer these FAQs instantly, providing directions via Google Maps and list of accepted insurance partners. This frees your staff to handle complex patient needs and emergency escalations.

M-Pesa Integration for Consultation Deposits

One of the most effective ways to reduce "fake" bookings is to collect a commitment fee. We integrate the M-Pesa Daraja API directly into your WhatsApp flow. When a patient selects a slot, the bot triggers an STK push for the deposit. Once paid, the appointment is automatically confirmed in your system and the patient receives their digital receipt—zero manual work required.
