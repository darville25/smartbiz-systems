Common Questions

WhatsApp Automation FAQ — Kenya

What Kenyan business owners ask before setting up their assistant.

How much does WhatsApp automation cost in Kenya?

One-time setup ranges from KES 50,000 for our 3-flow Starter package, KES 100,000 for our 10-flow Growth package, and KES 200,000+ for Premium enterprise setups. Monthly hosting maintenance fees start at KES 10,000.

How does the M-Pesa integration work on WhatsApp?

We hook directly into Safaricom Daraja API. When a user requests a booking, product, or commitment deposit, your bot requests an STK push. The customer enters their PIN directly on their phone screen, prompting immediate transaction settlement.

Do I need to leave my phone connected to run this?

No. We build directly on the official Meta WhatsApp Business Cloud API. Your chatbot runs completely in the cloud 24/7, even if your phone is turned off or has no internet connection.

Can the assistant sync with my existing CRM?

Yes. We synchronize lead values, conversation threads, and payment status parameters directly to CRM platforms like HubSpot, Salesforce, active campaigns, or basic Google Sheets in real-time.

Can the bot run in Swahili?

Yes. Our systems process natural Swahili, English, or Sheng. It dynamically adjusts its language based on the initial inputs selected by your client.
