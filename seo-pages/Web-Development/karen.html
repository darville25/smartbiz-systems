Common Questions

Web Development FAQ — Karen

What Karen business owners ask before getting started.

How much does a website cost in Karen?

Website packages for Karen businesses start from KES 30,000 for a 5-page startup site, KES 75,000 for a corporate site with CMS, and KES 150,000+ for a full ecommerce store with M-Pesa and WooCommerce. All packages can be paid via M-Pesa and include mobile-first design, WhatsApp integration, and basic SEO.

How long does it take to build a website?

Startup websites (5 pages) are delivered in 5–7 days. Corporate sites with CMS take 10–14 days. Ecommerce sites with M-Pesa integration take 3–4 weeks. Everything is delivered remotely — no need to visit an office in Nairobi.

Do I own the website after it's built?

100%. You own the domain, the hosting account, and all website files. SmartBizSystems has zero lock-in — if you ever want to move to another provider, you take everything with you. We build it, you own it.

Can my website accept M-Pesa payments?

Yes. We integrate M-Pesa Daraja API into ecommerce and service websites — customers pay directly on your site and receive automatic confirmations. This is built into the KES 150,000 ecommerce package and available as an add-on for other packages.

Will my website show up on Google?

Every site we build ships with technical SEO foundations — proper meta tags, schema markup, mobile optimisation, and fast loading speed. For ongoing Google rankings, we recommend pairing your website with our SEO retainer packages starting from KES 40,000/mo.
