Our Checklist for a Mobile-Dominant Real Estate Website

Making a website mobile-friendly is more than just making it "shrink." It requires a ground-up approach to User Experience (UX) designed for the Kenyan mobile user. Here are the non-negotiables we build into every property portal:


Finger-Friendly Navigation

We ensure all buttons and property links have a minimum "tap target" size. No more accidental clicks on the wrong listing. Our menus are designed for one-handed operation—crucial for users on the move.

Adaptive Image Galleries

High-resolution photos of Lavington mansions are heavy. We use adaptive serving—sending smaller file sizes to phones and larger ones to desktops—ensuring your galleries load in under 2 seconds on Safaricom 4G.

Sticky WhatsApp CTAs

Real estate is about immediate conversation. We place a floating WhatsApp button in the "thumb zone" (bottom-right), allowing seekers to instantly enquire about a property the moment they see something they like.

Google's Mobile-First Indexing: Why Your Desktop Site Doesn't Rank

Since 2019, Google has switched to Mobile-First Indexing. This means Google predominantly uses the mobile version of a site's content, crawled with the smartphone agent, for indexing and ranking. If your property website in Kilimani has a beautiful desktop version but a broken mobile version, Google will penalize you. You will disappear from the first page for terms like "apartments for rent in Kilimani" or "office space Lavington."


For real estate agencies, this is a crisis. Most leads in this sector are generated by ranking at the top of local searches. Mobile FAQ

Common Questions for Kilimani Agencies

Can you convert my existing desktop site to mobile-friendly?

In most cases, yes. We can implement a "Responsive Retrofit" where we rewrite your site's CSS (styling) to detect screen sizes and adjust the layout. However, if your current site is very old, a mobile-first rebuild is often more cost-effective and provides a better user experience for Kilimani property seekers.

Will my site work on older phones like older TECNO or Infinix models?

Absolutely. We use "Progressive Enhancement" techniques. This means we ensure the core functionality—viewing properties and contacting you via WhatsApp—works on any browser, from high-end iPhones used in Lavington to older Android devices common across Nairobi.

Do I need a separate mobile app (APK) for my agency?

For 95% of Kilimani agencies, no. A high-performance, mobile-responsive website (often called a PWA) is better because it doesn't require a download. People rarely download an app to find one apartment, but they will happily use a fast mobile website.
